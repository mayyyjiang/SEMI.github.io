### 📋 Struktur Biaya

Biaya SAP dirangkum menjadi satu angka: **“One Fee” Marketing Budget** — persentase berjenjang per kategori level 2 dan seller tier, yang terdiri dari tiga komponen transparan di bawah ini.

```
“One Fee” Marketing Budget  =  Ads TR  +  Affiliate Commission TR  +  Bundle Fee To Config
```

| | Komponen | Penjelasan |
| :--: | --- | --- |
| ① | **Ads TR** | Porsi take rate yang dialokasikan untuk **iklan & akuisisi trafik**, dieksekusi langsung oleh tim SAP TikTok Shop. |
| ② | **Affiliate Commission TR** | Porsi take rate untuk **komisi afiliasi / kreator**, ditentukan berdasarkan kategori produk level 2. |
| ③ | **Bundle Fee To Config** | Porsi take rate yang dikonfigurasi untuk **paket program promosi** (Bonus Cashback, Voucher Xtra/XBP, Mega Diskon, subsidi campaign). |
| **=** | **“One Fee” Marketing Budget** | **Total anggaran pemasaran** = ① + ② + ③. Satu angka inilah yang menjadi acuan biaya Anda. |

> 💰 **Tanpa pembayaran di muka (no upfront payment).** Biaya dipotong otomatis dari pendapatan pada setiap transaksi yang berhasil. Penyelesaian dilakukan sistem dan dana langsung masuk ke **Shop Wallet** Anda.

**▸ Tiga Jenjang Rate (berdasarkan Seller Tier)**

| Jenjang | Seller Tier | Keterangan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **BASIC** | T1 – T3 | Jenjang awal untuk seller yang baru mulai tumbuh |
| **TIER 1** | T4 – T5 | Rate menyesuaikan skala toko yang sedang berkembang |
| **TIER 2** | T6 – T9 | Jenjang untuk toko berskala besar |

🔍 **Cek rate kategori Anda** pada tabel lengkap: [Rate per Kategori / Category Rate Table](#-rate-per-kategori--category-rate-table) — 30 kategori level 1, 247 kategori level 2.

**Catatan penting**

1. Semua angka adalah **take rate (TR)** dalam persen, dipotong otomatis dari pendapatan per transaksi berhasil.
2. **“One Fee” Marketing Budget** selalu sama dengan penjumlahan Ads TR + Affiliate Commission TR + Bundle Fee To Config pada kategori dan jenjang yang sama.
3. Kategori toko untuk menentukan rate dilihat dari **produk terbanyak yang dijual** di toko (kategori level 2).
4. **Seller tier** (T1–T9) ditetapkan oleh tim SAP berdasarkan skala toko Anda; tim kami akan mengonfirmasi jenjang Anda saat onboarding.
5. Rate mengacu pada dokumen kerja resmi program dan dapat diperbarui oleh platform.

### 📋 Fee Structure

SAP costs are consolidated into a single number: the **“One Fee” Marketing Budget** — a tiered percentage by L2 category and seller tier, made up of the three transparent components below.

```
“One Fee” Marketing Budget  =  Ads TR  +  Affiliate Commission TR  +  Bundle Fee To Config
```

| | Component | Description |
| :--: | --- | --- |
| ① | **Ads TR** | The take-rate portion allocated to **advertising & traffic acquisition**, executed directly by the TikTok Shop SAP team. |
| ② | **Affiliate Commission TR** | The take-rate portion for **affiliate / creator commission**, set by L2 product category. |
| ③ | **Bundle Fee To Config** | The take-rate portion configured for the **bundled promotion programs** (Bonus Cashback, Voucher Xtra/XBP, Mega Discount, campaign subsidies). |
| **=** | **“One Fee” Marketing Budget** | **Total marketing budget** = ① + ② + ③. This single number is your cost reference. |

> 💰 **No upfront payment required.** Fees are automatically deducted from revenue per successful transaction. Settlement is system-driven and funds land directly in your **Shop Wallet**.

**▸ Three Rate Tiers (based on Seller Tier)**

| Tier | Seller Tier | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **BASIC** | T1 – T3 | Entry tier for sellers starting to scale |
| **TIER 1** | T4 – T5 | Rates aligned to a growing store scale |
| **TIER 2** | T6 – T9 | Tier for large-scale stores |

🔍 **Check your category rate** in the full table: [Rate per Kategori / Category Rate Table](#-rate-per-kategori--category-rate-table) — 30 L1 categories, 247 L2 categories.

**Important notes**

1. All figures are **take rates (TR)** in percent, automatically deducted from revenue per successful transaction.
2. The **“One Fee” Marketing Budget** always equals Ads TR + Affiliate Commission TR + Bundle Fee To Config for the same category and tier.
3. The store category used to determine the rate is based on the **product category with the most products sold** in the store (L2 category).
4. Your **seller tier** (T1–T9) is assigned by the SAP team based on your store scale; our team confirms your tier during onboarding.
5. Rates follow the program's official working document and may be updated by the platform.
